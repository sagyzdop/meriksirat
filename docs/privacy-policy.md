# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 1, 2026
**Last Updated:** November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Meriksirat equipment
booking and photo album platform ("Platform"), operated for NU Image
("Organization"), collects, why, who it is shared with, and what choices you
have. It complements the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md).

## 1. Who We Are

The Platform is run by NU Image, a student photography club at Nazarbayev
University. Contact: `nu_image@nu.edu.kz`, or the administrators through the
Platform or Telegram.

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 When You Sign In With Google

- Your Google account name, email address, profile picture and Google account
  ID.

### 2.2 When You Join the Club (Club Members)

Only people who enter the club code are asked for these.

- First and last name, date of birth, NU ID, major, graduation year and,
  optionally, Instagram username.
- Your Telegram chat ID and username, once you link the Telegram bot.
- Whether you want birthday congratulations.

### 2.3 While You Use the Platform

- Bookings, equipment usage, cancellations and overdue records.
- Photos you upload to albums, and photos you send to the Telegram bot when
  returning equipment.
- Session records: the IP address and browser user agent of each signed-in
  session.
- Your club status, role and clearance level, set by administrators.

### 2.4 Flamekeepers (#savenuimg)

If you choose to lend Google Drive storage as a flamekeeper:

- A Google authorization for the `drive.file` permission only, stored as an
  offline refresh token so the Platform can manage its files when you are not
  signed in.
- Your pledged storage, the storage the Platform uses in your Drive, your
  expected graduation date, and whether you appear on the leaderboard under
  your name or anonymously.

## 3. What We Access in Your Google Account

- **Sign-in:** your basic profile and email address only.
- **Flamekeepers:** with `drive.file`, the Platform can see and manage only
  the files and folders it created in your Drive. It cannot see, read or change
  any other file in your Drive. It creates album folders, uploads club photos
  into them, makes those folders viewable by anyone with the link, and moves or
  deletes them. It also reads how much storage your Drive has free.

The Platform does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it,
and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

**Meriksirat's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to
any other app will adhere to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.**

## 4. How We Use Your Data

- To run bookings: availability, reminders, cancellations of bookings nobody
  picked up, overdue handling and equipment accountability.
- To host albums, including storing album photos in flamekeepers' Drives.
- To send notifications by Telegram and, for flamekeepers, by email.
- To add member birthdays to the club calendar, if you opted in.
- To show the #savenuimg leaderboard.
- To keep the Platform secure and stop abuse, for example by rate limiting.

## 5. What Is Public

- **Album photos are public.** Every album's Drive folders are viewable by
  anyone with the link, whether or not the album is shared in the Platform.
  Do not upload photos you would not want to be public.
- The leaderboard shows flamekeepers' names and contributed storage, unless a
  flamekeeper chooses to appear anonymously.
- Album activity and booking events are posted to the club's Telegram audit
  channel, which club administrators can read.

## 6. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell personal data. We use these service providers to run the
Platform:

- **Cloudflare:** hosting, the database, file and cache storage.
- **Google:** sign-in, Google Drive (album storage, including flamekeepers'
  Drives) and Google Calendar (equipment and birthday calendars).
- **Telegram:** notifications and equipment returns.

We may also disclose data where Kazakh law or Nazarbayev University
regulations require it.

## 7. Retention

- **Accounts that are not members:** you may delete your account once any
  flamekeeping has stopped and your volumes have been moved off your Drive. If
  you sign up and then do nothing (no club membership, no Drive connection and
  nothing saved), the account is deleted after 7 days. Accounts with an
  `@alumni.nu.edu.kz` email are not deleted this way.
- **Members:** accounts and their records are kept indefinitely, so that there
  is always a record of who was responsible for each piece of equipment (see
  Terms of Service, Section 3.2).
  Your Drive authorization is deleted when the account is.
- **Album photos:** kept until the album expires (by default two years after
  the event), unless an administrator pins it or it is deleted earlier. After
  deletion, only the album's title and the reason it was deleted remain.
- **Sessions** expire automatically. Telegram return-flow session data expires
  within an hour.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

- You may ask to see or correct your personal data by contacting an
  administrator.
- Flamekeepers may stop lending storage at any time from their account page,
  or revoke the Platform's Drive access at
  [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  Revoking access without opting out means the albums stored in your Drive may
  be lost.
- You may turn off birthday congratulations and hide your name on the
  leaderboard.
- You have the rights given to you by the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan
  "On Personal Data and Their Protection".

## 9. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, and credentials are kept on the server, never in
your browser. No system is perfectly secure, and the Organization is not
responsible for the security of data held by Google, Cloudflare or Telegram.

## 10. Changes

We will announce material changes through the Platform or Telegram, and update
the dates above.

## 11. Contact

Questions or requests: `nu_image@nu.edu.kz`, or the administrators through
the Platform or Telegram.
