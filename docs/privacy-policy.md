# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 4, 2026
**Last Updated:** November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Meriksirat equipment
booking and photo album platform ("Platform"), operated for NU Image
("Organization"), collects, why, who it is shared with, and what choices you
have. It complements the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md).

The policy has three parts:

- **Part A** applies to everyone with an account.
- **Part B** applies to club members, who book equipment and create albums.
- **Part C** applies to flamekeepers, who pledge part of their Google Drive.

You may be a club member, a flamekeeper, both, or neither. Part A always
applies.

---

# Part A: Everyone

## 1. Who We Are

The Platform is run by NU Image, a student photography club at Nazarbayev
University. Contact: `nu_image@nu.edu.kz`, or the administrators through the
Platform or Telegram.

## 2. Data We Collect

- Your Google account name, email address, profile picture and Google account
  ID, when you sign in with Google.
- The date your account was created, which we keep as the date you accepted the
  Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy by logging in.
- Session records: the IP address and browser user agent of each signed-in
  session.
- Your photo favorites.
- Your role and clearance level, set by administrators.
- If an administrator bans your account: the ban, its reason and who set it.
  A message about the ban is also posted to the club's Telegram audit channel.
  Banned accounts are not deleted, and you cannot sign in or sign up again with
  the same Google account. Lifting a ban clears these details.

## 3. What We Access in Your Google Account

- **Sign-in:** your basic profile and email address only.
- **Club storage:** album photos are stored on Google accounts run by the club.
  We do not use your own Google Drive unless you choose to become a flamekeeper
  (Part C).

The Platform does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it,
and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

**Meriksirat's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to
any other app will adhere to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.**

## 4. What Is Public

- **Album photos are public.** Every album's Drive folders are viewable by
  anyone with the link, and every album is listed in the Platform's public
  gallery. Do not upload photos you would not want to be public.
- Album and booking activity is posted to the club's Telegram audit channel,
  which club administrators can read.

## 5. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell personal data. We use these service providers to run the
Platform:

- **Cloudflare:** hosting, the database, file and cache storage.
- **Google:** sign-in, Google Drive (album storage on club-run Google
  accounts), Google Calendar (equipment and birthday calendars), and Gmail for
  email notices.
- **Telegram:** notifications and equipment returns.

We may also disclose data where Kazakh law or Nazarbayev University
regulations require it.

## 6. Retention

- **Accounts that are not members:** you may delete your account once any
  flamekeeping has stopped and any photos the Platform stored in your Drive
  have been moved. Deleting it removes your sessions, linked Google accounts,
  Telegram link, favorites and flamekeeper record. Accounts are never deleted
  for being unfinished.
- **Album photos:** kept until the album is deleted. Albums are not deleted
  automatically. An administrator may schedule an album for deletion with notice
  (14 days by default), and its owner or an administrator may delete it at any
  time. When an album is deleted, its folder and photos are deleted, with no
  backup copy. A page saying why remains only when an album's Google Drive folder
  was permanently deleted and an administrator removed the album.
- **Sessions** expire automatically. Telegram return-flow session data expires
  within an hour.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

- You may ask to see or correct your personal data by contacting an
  administrator.
- You have the rights given to you by the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan
  "On Personal Data and Their Protection".

## 8. Security, Changes and Contact

Data is sent over HTTPS, and credentials are kept on the server, never in your
browser. No system is perfectly secure, and the Organization is not responsible
for the security of data held by Google, Cloudflare or Telegram.

We will announce material changes through the Platform or Telegram, and update
the dates above.

Questions or requests: `nu_image@nu.edu.kz`, or the administrators through the
Platform or Telegram.

# Part B: Club Members

Part B applies if you unlocked membership with the club code.

## 1. Data We Collect

- First and last name, date of birth, NU ID, major, graduation month and,
  optionally, Instagram username.
- Your Telegram chat ID and username, once you link the Telegram bot.
- Whether you want birthday congratulations.
- Bookings, equipment usage, cancellations and overdue records, including
  no-shows.
- Photos you upload to albums, and photos you send to the Telegram bot when
  returning equipment.
- Your member status (Active, Inactive or Alumni), set by administrators.

## 2. How We Use It

- To run bookings: availability, reminders, cancellations of bookings nobody
  picked up, overdue handling and equipment accountability.
- To host albums, including storing album photos on club-run Google accounts.
- To send notifications by Telegram and, for some accounts, by email.
- To add member birthdays to the club calendar, if you opted in.
- To keep the Platform secure and stop abuse, for example by rate limiting.

## 3. Who Sees It

Administrators and managers can see members' details, bookings and violations.
Booking and album activity is posted to the Telegram audit channel (Part A,
Section 4). Equipment and birthday calendars are kept in Google Calendar.

## 4. Retention

Member accounts and their records are kept indefinitely, so that there is
always a record of who was responsible for each piece of equipment (see Terms of
Service, Part B, Section 4.3). Members cannot delete their accounts.

## 5. Your Choices

- You may turn off birthday congratulations.
- You may ask an administrator to see or correct your data, or to evaluate
  deactivating your account. Accountability records may still be kept.

# Part C: Flamekeepers

Part C applies if you choose to pledge Google Drive storage as a flamekeeper
(#savenuimg). Flamekeeping is optional, and you do not need to be a club member.

## 1. Data We Collect

- Your permission for the Platform to use your Google Drive for this purpose,
  kept as an authorization (refresh token) on the server, never in your
  browser.
- Your pledged storage, the storage the Platform uses in your Drive, and the
  amount of your Drive in use, which we check regularly to apply your pledge
  and the club's per-account cap.
- Your expected graduation month, and the dates you last confirmed it.
- Your flamekeeper status (for example Active, Full, Draining, Lost or
  Stopped), and when we last reached your Drive.
- If your Google email changes, we update the email on your account.

## 2. What We Access in Your Google Account

- The Platform asks only for access to the files and folders it creates in your
  Drive (`drive.file`), plus your basic profile and email address. It does not
  read your other files.
- It checks how much of your Drive storage is in use.
- It needs room for your pledge: connecting is refused if your Drive has less
  free space than your pledge under the per-account cap.

## 3. Photos in Your Drive

New album photos go to the club's account, not your Drive. Photos are placed in
your Drive only after an administrator moves an album's photos to flamekeepers,
which is never automatic. Those photos are public like every album's photos, and
the folder holding them is shared with the club's account, so the club can copy
them back to its own storage, even if you can no longer be reached. They are
copied back, and deleted from your Drive, when an administrator moves them, when
the album is deleted, or when your flamekeeping ends.

## 4. Emails

We may email you about your flamekeeping: when your Drive access is lost, when
your graduation month needs confirming, and when your flamekeeping ends.

## 5. Retention

- Your Drive authorization is removed when your flamekeeping ends (once any
  photos have moved back to the club) and when your account is deleted.
- If you revoke Drive access while holding photos, you are marked Lost and have
  a grace period (7 days by default) to reconnect. The Platform copies the
  photos to the club's account meanwhile, and your original files stay in your
  Drive until you reconnect, then they are deleted.

## 6. Your Choices

- Stop lending storage at any time from your My Account page. Your pledge ends
  at once if the Platform holds no photos of yours, otherwise once they are back
  on the club's account.
- Revoke the Platform's Drive access at
  [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  If you hold no photos, this affects no album.
- Change your pledge or graduation month at any time from My Account.
