Albums have no fixed lifetime and are not deleted automatically. An album stays

- **Scheduled deletion.** An administrator may schedule an album for deletion,

  deleted from Google Drive, the photos cannot be recovered, and an administrator
  may remove the album.

Albums that are more than two years past their event are marked in the Platform
as ones that may be deleted if storage runs low.

A deleted album's folder and photos are removed, and there is no backup copy. In
the third case the album's address keeps a page that says the folder is gone;
after a scheduled or manual deletion nothing remains.

### 3.4 Storage Limits

# Part C: Flamekeepers

Part C applies if you become a flamekeeper (#savenuimg) by pledging part of
your `@nu.edu.kz` Google Drive storage to the Platform. Flamekeeping is
optional. Signing up never requires connecting a Drive, and you can become a
flamekeeper later from the Become a flamekeeper page. You do not need to be a
club member to be a flamekeeper, and being one does not make you a member.

## 1. Becoming and Ending Flamekeeping

- **Connecting.** You connect the Google Drive account with the same
  `@nu.edu.kz` address you signed in with. Connecting your Drive means you agree
  to these Terms. The Platform asks only for access to the files it creates
  (`drive.file`).
- **Enough room.** Your Drive must have room for your pledge under the club's
  per-account cap (20 GB by default). If it does not, the connection is refused
  until you free up space or pledge less.
- **Graduation.** You give your expected graduation month, which must be in the
  future. You are asked to confirm it once a year. Flamekeeping ends
  automatically 60 days before it.
- **Ending.** Flamekeeping also ends if you stop it, if your email stops being
  `@nu.edu.kz`, or if you are banned. If the Platform holds none of your
  Drive's photos, it ends at once. Otherwise you are Draining until the photos
  are back on the club's account, and you need to do nothing.

## 2. Your Pledge

Your pledge is the most storage the Platform may use in your Drive. It must be
at least 512 MB while you are flamekeeping. It is a cap on what the Platform
uses, not space set aside in Google. You can change it at any time, or stop
flamekeeping instead. If your Drive or your pledge has no room left, you are
marked Full until room frees up.

## 3. Photos in Your Drive

- **Optional reserve space.** New album photos go to the club's account, not
  your Drive. Photos are placed in your Drive only after an administrator moves
  an album's photos to flamekeepers, and never automatically. Until then, your
  pledge is reserve space.
- **Ownership.** Photos the Platform stores in your Drive are owned by you in
  Google's terms, and count against your Google storage, up to your pledge. The
  Organization keeps its license to use them as described in Section 3.2 of
  Part B.
- **Shared folders.** The folder holding the photos is shared with the club's
  account, so the club can copy the photos back, even if you can no longer be
  reached.
- **No editing or deleting.** You must not edit, rename, move or delete the
  files the Platform stores in your Drive. The Platform manages them for you.
- **Moves and deletion.** The Platform may move photos out of your Drive, and
  delete them from it, when an administrator moves an album, when an album is
  deleted, or when you stop flamekeeping.
- **Revoking access.** You may revoke the Platform's Drive access at any time at
  Google. If you hold photos when you revoke, you are marked Lost and have a
  grace period (7 days by default) to reconnect. During that time the Platform
  copies the photos to the club's account, and your original files stay in your
  Drive until you reconnect, then they are deleted. If you hold no photos,
  revoking affects no album.
- **Your other files.** The Platform only works with the files it creates in
  your Drive and does not read the rest of it. It checks how much of your Drive
  is in use, to apply your pledge and the cap.

## 4. Notices

The Platform may email you about your flamekeeping: when your Drive access is
lost, when your graduation month needs confirming, and when flamekeeping ends.
