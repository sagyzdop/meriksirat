# Membership and flamekeeping are separate capabilities

One user row can be a member, a flamekeeper, or both. Membership is a flag that
gates booking, album creation and the `manager`/`admin` roles. It is unlocked by
entering the current club code, which admins can see and rotate (rotation never
affects existing members; there is no rate limit). Users who existed before the
split became members automatically. Flamekeeping is a separate record carrying the
pledge and the Drive grant: members switch it on from their account page, and
flamekeeper-only users sign up for it directly. A flamekeeper can later become a
member by entering the code. Membership is permanent: members can't delete their
own account and admins can't delete users. Flamekeeper-only users may opt out and
delete their account once their volumes have drained.
